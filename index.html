Frequently Asked Questions

Health questions, answered.

We make healthcare easy to understand and easier to access.

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

No referral is required for most departments. You can book directly with our front desk or online appointment form.

Can I book same-day appointments?

Yes, same-day appointments are available based on doctor availability and clinic schedule.

Can I visit a specialist without a prior consultation?

Patients can request a specialist consultation directly. Our support team can help guide the right department.

Do you support WhatsApp appointment support?

Yes, our teams can help coordinate appointments and answer general health support questions via WhatsApp.
